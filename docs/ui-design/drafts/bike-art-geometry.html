Default bike pictures from real geometry

Each picture is computed from a real reference bike's size M frame numbers and drawn in the thin-line style. The type shows through proportions, not decoration: the mountain bike's long, slack front and fat tyres; the road bikes' short wheelbase and thin tyres; the city bike's mudguards and rack. All five share one scale.
